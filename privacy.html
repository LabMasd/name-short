name.short — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 June 2026

Short version: name.short collects nothing, sends nothing, and stores nothing off your device.

What data we collect

None. name.short does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data or usage data. There are no analytics, no trackers, no remote servers, and no accounts.

How the extension works

  • All processing is local. Names you type, or text you select and shorten, are converted to slugs entirely inside your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Settings storage. Your chosen options (case, strategy, max length, date, ascii) are saved with chrome.storage.local on your own device so the popup remembers them. This data never leaves your machine and can be cleared by removing the extension.
  • Clipboard. When you click copy or use the right-click menu, the resulting slug is written to your system clipboard. name.short never reads your clipboard.

Permissions & why they are used

  • contextMenus — adds the right-click "Shorten … → copy slug" item.
  • storage — remembers your settings locally.
  • scripting / activeTab — only when you click the right-click menu, to copy the slug to the clipboard on the current page. No page content is read or sent.
  • notifications — shows a small "Copied: …" confirmation.

Third parties

name.short does not share data with any third party because it has no data to share.
